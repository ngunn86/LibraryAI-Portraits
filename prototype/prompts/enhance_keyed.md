# enhance_keyed v1 (PIP-13 + PIP-03)

Send: the descreened original (as an image), then this text. Ask for the closest available aspect ratio to the original, and the largest output up to 2K.

---

This is an old photograph of a real historical person. It will become a small black-and-white dot portrait, so the face must stay exactly theirs.

Make a faithful, subtle restoration:
- Remove printing dot patterns, film grain, scratches, dust, stains, creases and compression blocks.
- Gently clarify detail that is already there: the eyes, eyebrows, the hairline and hair texture, the lips, and the edges of the collar and clothing.
- Even out uneven fading, but keep the photograph's natural light and shadow.

Keep the following exactly as in the original, with nothing added, removed or improved:
- Identity and face shape: eye shape, nose, mouth, jaw, ears, skin texture, wrinkles and age.
- Expression, head angle, gaze direction, hairstyle and hair outline.
- Clothing and pose.
- Framing: the same position and size of the person in the picture. Do not crop, zoom, rotate, flip, extend or recentre.

Do not beautify, smooth skin, change age, open or close eyes, add teeth, add or remove glasses, jewellery or facial hair, or invent detail that cannot be seen in the original. If something is unclear, leave it soft rather than guessing.

Render the person in black and white (neutral grey, no sepia or colour tint).

Replace everything behind the person with one flat, solid, pure green (#00FF00): no gradient, shadow, glow, texture or border. Keep the outline of the hair and shoulders natural, and don't paint green into the hair. The person's edges must touch the green directly.
